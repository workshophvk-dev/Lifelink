The problem we solve

Every day, patients in India lose precious time finding compatible blood or organ donors. Existing platforms are static directories: they depend on manual search, ignore urgency, cannot predict shortages, and lose donors after one donation.

LifeLink applies AI to connect donors and recipients, forecast demand and keep donors engaged, while medical data stays encrypted and is shared only with certified healthcare providers.

Objectives

  • Cut donor-recipient matching time with an AI ranking model
  • Predict regional blood demand to prevent shortages
  • Improve donor retention with personalised engagement
  • Protect privacy with encryption and tamper-evident records

System architecture and tech stack

Tech stack

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Planned backend: Node.js or Python Flask
  • AI: scikit-learn for forecasting, LLM API for chatbot
  • Database: PostgreSQL with encryption
  • Maps: Leaflet with OpenStreetMap (planned)
  • In this demo: Web Crypto (AES-GCM, SHA-256), Web Speech API, QR codes, in-browser machine learning

Impact and future scope

  • Supports UN SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being
  • Train the model on real hospital data
  • SMS and WhatsApp alerts
  • Link with India's e-RaktKosh blood bank network
  • Mobile app with voice support

Frequently asked questions

Is donating blood safe?

Yes. New sterile equipment is used for every donor, and about 350 to 450 ml is taken.

Who can see my medical data?

Only certified healthcare providers involved in your donation.

Does AI decide who gets blood?

No. AI ranks and suggests. Doctors make the final decision.

Private by design

Medical details are encrypted and shared only with certified healthcare providers.

How AI is used safely

  • Anomaly checks flag suspicious logins and repeated fake requests.
  • Uploaded medical documents are checked before a request goes live.
  • AI ranks and suggests. Healthcare professionals make the final call.
  • This demo uses synthetic data only.
